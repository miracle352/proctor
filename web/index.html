An AI earns it in a sealed exam. Every wallet that lets it pay checks it first. Anyone who catches it paying a scam ends it, and the trap that caught it becomes part of the exam for every AI after.

  1. It earns the licence in a sealed exam

    42 payment requests, half of them scams, locked so nobody can read them, us included. They open only inside secure hardware, where the AI answers. Pass with 90% and no scam paid.

  2. Anyone can set a trap for it

    A fake invoice, a bank change slipped into a thread, an approval for the wrong amount. Your browser seals it and Proctor puts it to every licensed AI. Code, not people, checks that your trap really breaks a rule.

  3. Caught means cut off, everywhere

    The licence is suspended on NEAR in the same step. Every wallet that checks it stops paying, the money goes back to its owner, and the trap joins the exam as a known scam.

Questions people ask

Who is this for?

Teams that let an AI agent pay bills or move money, and want proof it can tell a scam from a real invoice. Contracts that pay on an AI's behalf, which check the licence with one call. And anyone who likes catching scams: hunters, people or AI agents, earn what a caught AI paid.

What happens when an AI is caught?

Its licence is suspended on NEAR in the same transaction. Every wallet that checks the licence refuses its next payment and returns what's left to the owner. When the hunter reveals the trap, the licence is revoked for good and the trap becomes a known scam every AI must pass.

Who decides whether a trap is fair?

Code. Each trap is built from parts (sender, account, amount, approvals), and the contract rebuilds it and checks it against the company's rules when it's revealed. An honest payment passed off as a scam cancels the hunt automatically, and the hunter's stake goes to the AIs.

Is the money real?

It's real NEAR on NEAR's test network, so it has no cash value, but every payment and every frozen wallet is a real transaction anyone can look up.

Can I test Claude, GPT or Gemini?

Yes, through NEAR AI Cloud. Those models run on their makers' servers, so the maker can see the questions, and each certificate records that. Qwen runs inside NEAR AI's secure hardware, where nobody sees them.

Do I need cryptocurrency?

No. Setting a trap needs no wallet at all. Licensing an agent needs a free NEAR testnet account.

Is it live?

Yes. Every number on this site is read from the contract when you open it. It hasn't had a security audit.
