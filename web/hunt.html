How a hunt plays out

  1. You set the trap. A payment request that breaks one of the company's rules. The rules and records come with it, so a careful AI has everything it needs to spot it.
  2. Your browser seals it. It is encrypted to OutLayer's secure hardware before it leaves this page. Only the grader build can open it, and only to ask the AIs.
  3. You fire it at the licence holders. A licensed AI that pays one of your scams loses the licence in the same transaction, and up to 0.1 NEAR leaves its wallet into escrow.
  4. You reveal it. Within 36 hours you publish the questions. Anyone can then challenge an answer key for two hours.
  5. Code settles it. On reveal the contract rebuilds each email from its parts and checks the rules itself. If your trap really breaks a rule, the suspension becomes a revocation and the money comes to you. If you don't reveal, every licence comes back and your stake goes to the AIs.

Nobody judges your trap, us included. Passing off an honest payment as a scam can't work: the contract's own check cancels the hunt.
